\*"Underwritten Net Operating Income"\* shall mean, as of any date of calculation, the sum of: (a) (i) the monthly gross Rents from Leases in effect as of such date from Tenants that are current on their rental obligations, annualized, adjusted for a vacancy factor equal to the greater of (x) actual vacancy, or (y) 5.00%, and (ii) the Operating Income (excluding Rents from Leases) for the trailing three-month actual total, annualized, minus (b) the sum of: (i) concessions based on a trailing three-month actual total, annualized, and (ii) Operating Expenses for the 12-month period ending on such date, assuming (x) base property management fees equal to the greater of (1) the actual amount paid by Borrower during such prior 12-month period, and (2) 3.00% of Gross Revenues, and (y) a normalized adjustment for Replacements equal to $200 per unit at the Property per annum, with any adjustments (increases or decreases) for Extraordinary Expenses and/or other nonrecurring items as reasonably determined by Agent actually incurred by Borrower for the period in question and/or offsets to expenses.



\*"Operating Income"\* shall mean, for any period, all income of Borrower during such period from the use, ownership or operation of the Property, including:



(a) all amounts collected by Borrower that are payable by any Person as Rent and other amounts under Leases or other agreements relating to the Property;



(b) business interruption insurance proceeds allocable to the applicable reporting period; and



(c) all other amounts which in accordance with GAAP, (or other acceptable accounting method, which may be tax based) are included in Borrower's annual financial statements as operating income attributable to the Property.



Notwithstanding the foregoing, Operating Income shall not include (a) any Insurance Proceeds (other than business interruption and/or rental loss insurance proceeds and only to the extent allocable to the applicable reporting period), (b) any proceeds resulting from the Transfer of all or any portion of the Property, (c) any Rent attributable to a Lease prior to the date in which the Tenant thereunder has taken occupancy or in which the actual payment of rent is required to commence thereunder, (d) security deposits received from Tenants until forfeited or applied, or (e) any Lease termination payments other than penalties payable by tenants under the Leases.



\*"Operating Expenses"\* shall mean, for any period, without duplication, all expenses actually paid or payable by Borrower during such period in connection with the operation, management, maintenance, repair and use of the Property, computed in accordance with GAAP. Operating Expenses specifically shall include (i) all expenses incurred in the immediately preceding twelve (12)-month period, (ii) property management fees in an amount equal to the greater of (A) the management fees actually paid under the Management Agreement, and (B) 3.00% of Gross Revenues, (iii) administrative, payroll, security and general expenses for the Property, (iv) the cost of utilities, inventories and fixed asset supplies consumed in the operation of the Property, (v) advertising expenses, and (vi) Taxes and Other Charges (other than income taxes or Other Charges in the nature of income taxes) and insurance premiums. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Operating Expenses shall not include (1) depreciation or amortization, (2) income taxes, (3) Capital Expenditures, and (4) Debt Service.
