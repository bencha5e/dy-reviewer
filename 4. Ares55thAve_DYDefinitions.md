\*"Net Operating Income"\* shall mean, with respect to any Individual Property, as of any date of calculation and for the applicable period: the sum of (a) annualized Operating Income based current monthly base rent and additional rent, including tenant recoveries or reimbursements from existing tenants that are in occupancy of their space, excluding (i) Tenants that are 60-days past due on base rent, (ii) month-to-month tenants (iii) Tenants that have filed for bankruptcy protection and the applicable Lease has not been reaffirmed, (iv) non-credit tenants, as determined by Administrative Agent, that have "gone dark", (v) credit tenants as determined by Administrative Agent, with a remaining lease term that is less than two (2) years beyond the fully extended Term of the applicable Mortgage Loan that have "gone dark", (vi) Tenants that have notified in writing of their intent to vacate and (vii) non-recurring/extraordinary categories of other income, plus (b) annualized revenue to be received from Tenants that have newly executed Leases but have not yet taken occupancy to the extent occupancy is expected to occur within 90-days or 180-days for investment grade (BBB- or higher) tenants, and subject to clause (2) below, including the rental value of any free rent period, plus (c) other income based on the most recent 12-month period adjusted for any non-recurring/extraordinary expenses, but including business interruption insurance proceeds, minus (d) actual operating expenses based on the most recent trailing 12-month period, adjusted for (i) management fees at the greater of (x) actual or (y) 3.0% of gross operating income, non-recurring/extraordinary expenses and $0.10 per square feet replacement reserve. The revenue described under (a) and (b) shall also be adjusted for (1) a vacancy factor of the greater of (x) actual vacancy or (y) 5% and (2) any Tenant currently in a free rent period that exceeds 1.0 months per lease year, whereby such Tenant's revenue shall be adjusted by not including the revenue equivalent of free rent that exceeds 1.0 months per lease year. If any Mortgage Loan Annex shall contain a different definition of Net Operating Income, such definition shall control with respect to the applicable Mortgage Loan. All calculations and determinations should be reasonably determined in good faith by Administrative Agent.



\*"Operating Income"\* shall mean, in respect of an Individual Property for any period, all income of Borrower during such period from the use, ownership or operation of the Individual Property, including:

(a) all amounts payable to Borrower by any Person as Rent and other amounts under Leases or other agreements relating to the Individual Property;

(b) business interruption insurance proceeds allocable to the applicable reporting period; and

(c) all other amounts which in accordance with the Accounting Principles are included in Borrower's annual financial statements as operating income attributable to the Individual Property.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, Operating Income shall not include (a) any Insurance Proceeds (other than business interruption and/or rental loss insurance proceeds and only to the extent allocable to the applicable reporting period), (b) any proceeds resulting from the Transfer of all or any portion of the Individual Property, (c) any Rent attributable to a Lease prior to the date in which the Tenant thereunder has taken occupancy or in which the actual payment of rent is required to commence thereunder, (d) any item of income otherwise included in Operating Income but paid directly by any Tenant to a Person other than Borrower as an offset or deduction against Rent payable by such Tenant; provided such item of income is for payment of an item of expense (such as payments for utilities paid directly to a utility company) and such expense is otherwise excluded from the definition of Operating Expenses pursuant to clause (6) of the definition thereof, (e) security deposits received from Tenants until forfeited or applied, (f) any Lease termination payments other than penalties payable by tenants under the Leases, (g) any amount recovered in a litigation proceeding, or (h) any Rents paid by or on behalf of any Tenant under a Lease which is the subject of any proceeding or action relating to its bankruptcy, reorganization or other arrangement pursuant to the Bankruptcy Code or any similar federal or state law or which has been adjudicated a bankrupt or insolvent unless such Lease has been assumed by the trustee or been reaffirmed in such proceeding or action. Operating Income shall be calculated in accordance with the Accounting Principles based upon information provided to Administrative Agent by Borrower pursuant to Section 4.1.6 hereof and such shall be reasonably determined in good faith by Administrative Agent.
